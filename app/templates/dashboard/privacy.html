DATA HANDLING AND CONFIDENTIALITY AGREEMENT

FBS-Based Diabetes Risk Screening Program — City Health Office of Santa Rosa (LHU I / LHU II)

1. PURPOSE

This system exists to support community-based Fasting Blood Sugar (FBS) diabetes risk screening across LHU I and LHU II, the two stations operating under the City Health Office of Santa Rosa. It allows authorized health workers to record screening data, generate a preliminary risk classification, and route high-risk patients toward confirmatory testing and care, while giving City Health Office staff visibility into screening coverage and risk distribution across Santa Rosa's barangays.

2. WHAT DATA IS SHARED

Only the following categories are shared between LHU I and LHU II under the City Health Office:

  • De-identified screening results: FBS values, computed risk level (Low/Moderate/High), barangay, age range, and sex — used for aggregate reporting and resource planning.
  • Patient-identifying information (name, exact birthdate, contact details) is NOT shared beyond the station where the patient was originally screened, except where the patient is referred to Santa Rosa District Hospital or a DOH facility for confirmatory testing or follow-up care, and only to the extent necessary for that referral.
  • No data in this system is sold, licensed, or shared with commercial entities, insurers, or any party outside the City Health Office / DOH public-health reporting chain.

3. LEGAL BASIS

Data collection, processing, and handling under this program are conducted in accordance with the Data Privacy Act of 2012 (Republic Act No. 10173) and its Implementing Rules and Regulations, under the informed consent obtained from each patient at the point of screening, and under the protocol approved by the program's Institutional Review Board (IRB).

4. HOW DATA IS PROTECTED

  • Direct identifiers are separated from clinical variables before any aggregate or cross-station reporting takes place.
  • Access to identifiable patient records is restricted to the health workers and officers directly involved in that patient's screening and care, enforced by role-based access controls within the system.
  • All access to patient records is logged and auditable.

5. PATIENT RIGHTS

Patients screened under this program retain the right to access their own screening data, request correction of inaccurate information, and request deletion of their record, subject to any retention period required for public-health reporting or active care obligations.

6. RESPONSIBILITIES OF LHU I AND LHU II

Each station (LHU I, LHU II) and its staff agree to: use shared data solely for the purposes described above; not attempt to re-identify de-identified records; report any suspected data breach to the program administrator immediately; and ensure staff accessing the system are trained on these confidentiality obligations.

7. DURATION AND TERMINATION

This agreement remains in effect for the duration of the station's participation in the program and may be terminated by the City Health Office with written notice. Termination does not retroactively affect data already lawfully shared prior to termination.
