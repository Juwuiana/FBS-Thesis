Privacy Policy

The LHU Patient Portal collects and uses your health information only to support your diabetes risk screening at the Local Health Unit.

What we collect

Your name, contact details, address, medical and family history, lifestyle information, vital signs, fasting blood sugar (FBS) results, and any home readings you enter.

How we use it

To compute your risk level, show your screening history and trends, and let your health worker follow up with you. Your data may also be used in de-identified form for research.

Who can see it

Only you and authorized LHU health workers. We do not sell or share your personal information with third parties.

Your rights

Under the Data Privacy Act of 2012 (RA 10173), you may ask to access or correct your information, or raise a concern about how it is handled. Contact your LHU health worker to do so.

Security

Access to your record requires your login. Please do not share your password, and sign out on shared devices.

Help

Reading your results

My Results shows every screening, your latest risk level, and how your FBS changed since your last visit. Use the tabs to switch between history, adding a record, and your FBS trend.

Updating your information

Use Add New Record if your health details changed, or Edit on a past screening. Changes are reviewed by your health worker before they become final.

Home FBS readings

Home readings are for reference only. Your official FBS and risk level come from your screening at the LHU.

Need more help?

Visit or contact your LHU health worker. If you feel very unwell, seek medical care right away instead of waiting for a portal update.
